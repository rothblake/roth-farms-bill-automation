Roth Farms Bill Automation

Roth Farms Bill Automation is a private, internal tool used by Roth Farms to enter our own vendor bills into our own QuickBooks Online companies. It is not a public product and is not offered to or used by anyone outside our organization.

What it does: reads a bill document we provide, extracts the vendor, date, amount, and invoice number, and records it as a vendor bill in the correct QuickBooks Online company via Intuit's API.

Connect / reconnect: the tool connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's standard OAuth 2.0 authorization. To connect or reconnect, the owner runs the tool's authorization step on the local machine and approves access in the Intuit consent screen.

Disconnect: access can be revoked at any time from the Intuit account's Connected Apps settings, or by removing the locally stored tokens.
